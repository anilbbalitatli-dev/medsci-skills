## Scope
Respiratory Care publishes clinical and applied physiological research in respiratory therapy and critical care, aimed at respiratory therapists and clinicians managing the airway and the ventilator. Coverage spans mechanical ventilation and ventilator management, weaning and extubation, aerosol and drug delivery, oxygen therapy including high-flow nasal oxygen, noninvasive ventilation, airway clearance and secretion management, pulmonary function and bedside respiratory monitoring, and respiratory care education and practice. The journal is receptive to single-center physiological and device-focused studies whose findings change something at the bedside.

## Special Notes
Society journal of the AARC, with a readership of respiratory therapists rather than pulmonologists as researchers; a submission must state plainly what changes in bedside respiratory care if the finding holds. Original Research requires a **Quick Look** box with two fixed sub-headings (Current Knowledge; What This Paper Contributes to Our Knowledge), written for a reader deciding whether to read the paper rather than as a restatement of the abstract; omitting it or duplicating the abstract is a common avoidable rejection. Word allowance (5,000) is more generous than most competitor journals, so Methods can carry the ventilator settings and modes needed for reproduction.
